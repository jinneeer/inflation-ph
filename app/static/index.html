Advanced model settings

For analysts: SARIMA(p,d,q)(P,D,Q,12) on log CPI and an additive ETS model. Changing these re-fits the model live (about a second). The defaults are the settings validated in the backtest below.

Shaded bands are likely ranges (80% and 95%). Price changes use the 2018 basket-weight approximation.

How to read this: the dark line is the best guess; the bands show where inflation should land 80% and 95% of the time. The bands widen the further ahead we look. Pick a quick what-if above to see how a price jump shifts the whole path (dotted grey = no change).

Published backtest

Fold-level interpretation

Stable period (Nov 2024–Oct 2025): Holt-Winters has the smaller average miss, 0.84 vs 1.16 pp. Surge period (Nov 2025–Oct 2026): SARIMA degrades less, 3.03 vs 4.10 pp average miss. A single blended accuracy would hide this regime dependence, so both folds stay visible. MAPE is inflated near zero and is shown for completeness only.
